Speed measurement · calibrate for km/h

Without calibration, speed is measured in analysis pixels/second. For km/h, mark the four corners of a measured rectangular patch on the flat road: far-left → far-right → near-right → near-left. Enter its real width and length; do not guess from the video.

No calibration. Region estimates use px/s.

Calibrate the road once for all tracks. Only stable tracks inside the marked patch receive km/h estimates. Use a flat road, a near-fixed camera and real-time footage. Slow-motion or time-lapse footage cannot establish real vehicle speed from playback timestamps.

Incident review · optional

Review automatic vehicle tracks against rules you configure. A zebra crossing alone is not an offence. Signal conditions are supplied here; the app does not infer them from pixels.

One interval per line: start seconds, end seconds, condition (red, green, observe, stop_required or unknown). Uncovered times are unknown. Leave blank to use one condition for the whole clip.

Missed-stop review needs an observed entry into this approach and continuous calibrated speed. A stop means at least one second at or below 2 km/h within the approach.

A speeding candidate requires at least 0.5 seconds above your threshold plus a conservative review policy guard of 2 km/h or 5% of the threshold, whichever is larger. Borderline estimates are withheld. This is not a legal tolerance or validated measurement-accuracy bound.
