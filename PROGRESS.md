## Decisions and limitations

- Local URL: http://127.0.0.1:8511. No cloud deployment or paid services.
- PyAV supplements OpenCV to preserve variable frame timing. Output is H.264/AAC; encoding is lossy. Audio export includes the first track.
- Source timing comes from decoded presentation timestamps; inconsistent container duration metadata may differ from the actual final presentation endpoint.
- Colour masks use original colour values. Road candidates are geometric proposals, not semantic road labels or violations.
- Video masks are sampled approximately once per second; colour processing and measurements run for every frame.
- Original media and prior runs remain on disk; output is reserved for final submission files.
- AI integration is in progress; the browser app exposes both IVP and experimental AI image/video workflows.

### 24 September - trajectory calibration continuity

Hardened traffic_ai/trajectories.py before further automatic road-plane work. A change between calibrations (including calibrated to uncalibrated) now discards old heading history. ### 24 September - preserve the originating approach through a turn
